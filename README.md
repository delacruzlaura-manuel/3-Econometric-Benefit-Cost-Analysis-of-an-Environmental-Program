The project's objective is to estimate the true effect of temperature on monthly mortality rate among elder people using a panel data of the USA between 1960 and 2004.
Thus, it presents a descriptive analysis of the data, cross-sectional correlation, causal analysis, placebo test and a cost-benefit analysis of an
environmental program implementation. Regarding the last point, it is assummed the following statements: a value of a statistical life-year is $314,000, there is 
22,343,459 people aged 65 or older life in southern climate regions and 34,939,224 people aged 65 or older life in non-southern climate regions.
